## 3. Position on the "virtual enterprise" metaphor (opinion)

The metaphor is useful for planning and for the Enterprise view. It is risky as a mental model:

- **Agents are not employees.** They have no stake, no memory beyond what the user confirms, and no accountability. Calling them a "department" invites users to delegate _responsibility_, when they are only delegating _work_.
- **A real enterprise has slack, judgement and dissent built in by people.** Here dissent exists only where the design puts it: critics, disputes, the independent reviewer. Those must stay mandatory and visible.
- **Recommendation.** Keep the metaphor in the view layer (Enterprise view, expert tier). In the default experience say "your team of AI helpers works inside the limits you set; you stay responsible for what you approve". Put that sentence in the charter summary the Sponsor approves (R10).

## 4. Deskilling: a closer look

**Evidence:**

- Colonoscopy: unassisted adenoma detection fell from 28.4 % to 22.4 % after routine AI exposure. Observational, OR 0.69 (Budzyń et al. 2025).
- Coding: in a 52-person RCT, AI-assisted learners scored 17 % lower on comprehension, with debugging most affected. Learners who used AI for conceptual questions fared better than those who delegated generation (Shen & Tamkin, Anthropic 2026) [U on the per-pattern figures, which come from secondary coverage].

**Mechanism.** Bainbridge's irony: the more reliable the automation, the less practice humans get, and the worse they perform when it fails. This is exactly when Program mode escalates to them.

**Safeguards, in order of cost:**

1. **Explanations that teach** (cheap): each accepted deliverable carries a 3-line "why" in plain language. Hidden from experts by default.
2. **Learn mode** (R14): predict or write first, then compare.
3. **Escalations with context.** When Program mode asks a human, the card includes what the human needs to judge, not just yes or no: the evidence, the strongest dissent and the reversible alternative (R8).
4. **Periodic drills** (opinion; phase 7+): an optional "you review this one without the critic's verdict" round, scored against the canary truth (R9). This keeps review skill alive the way pilots keep manual flying skill.

## 5. What would make me recommend pausing Program mode

These are tripwires for the PO (opinion). If any one holds after phase 5 ships, pause new unattended portfolios until it is fixed:

- G5 is non-zero for any safety invariant;
- G3 shows an undetected stall in fault-injection tests;
- the G2 rubber-stamp rate exceeds 25 % on consequential approvals;
- the G4 escape rate of V2 deliverables is not lower than that of V1 (verification adds no value);
- any credible report of a user's provider account being suspended because of KP-driven usage.
